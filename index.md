# Privacy Policy for QuizTrivia Odyssey

**Last Updated: April 24, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how "QuizTrivia Odyssey" (the "App") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Automatically Collected Information
When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by third-party services integrated into the App:
*   **Device Information:** IP address, device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
*   **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the App (e.g., levels completed, diamonds earned).
*   **Advertising IDs:** Android Advertising ID (AAID) used for personalized advertising.

### B. Information You Provide
The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information like your name, email address, or phone number to play. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

## 2. Third-Party Services
The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Link to privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the App:
*   [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
*   [AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)
*   [Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy)

## 3. Advertising
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. 
*   **Personalized Ads:** Depending on your location (e.g., EEA/UK) and the consent you provided via our in-app consent form, Google may show personalized ads based on your interests.
*   **Rewarded Ads:** You may choose to watch video ads to earn in-game rewards (Diamonds).

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 5. Data Security
We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
*   **GDPR (EEA/UK users):** You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can manage your advertising consent at any time through the settings menu within the App.
*   **CCPA (California users):** You have the right to request what personal information is collected and to opt-out of the "sale" of personal information (which includes sharing data for personalized ads).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
